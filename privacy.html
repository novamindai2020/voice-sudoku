Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Voice Sudoku ("the app") is a Sudoku puzzle game. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Information we collect

None. The app does not require an account, and it does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, or any other personal data. We do not operate any servers that receive your data.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress, settings, and play statistics (such as games completed and best times) locally on your device so you can resume play. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Voice control, microphone, and speech recognition

The app offers optional voice control, so you can play by speaking numbers and commands. It only works if you grant Microphone and Speech Recognition permission, and it listens only while voice control is switched on during a game. You can revoke these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Speech is processed using Apple's Speech Recognition framework. Whenever your device supports it, recognition happens on your device and audio does not leave it. If on-device recognition is unavailable or does not return a result, iOS may send the audio to Apple to be transcribed, as described in Apple's Privacy Policy and the "Dictation & Privacy" information in iOS Settings.

The app does not record, save, or transmit audio itself, and we never receive your voice data. The only thing the app keeps is the recognized command (for example, a number or "undo"), which is acted on immediately, plus a simple note that voice control was used in a game.

Third parties, analytics, and advertising

The app does not include analytics, tracking, or third-party advertising, and we do not share any data with third parties. The only third-party processing is the optional Apple speech recognition described above.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect data from children of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
