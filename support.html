Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Fill every row, column, and 3×3 box with the digits 1–9 so that no digit repeats within any row, column, or box. Tap a cell, then tap a number on the pad.

How do I use voice control?

Tap the microphone button during a game and allow Microphone and Speech Recognition access when asked. Voice control is currently available in English (US). Speak clearly and keep background noise low for best results.

Voice control isn't working.

Open iOS Settings, find the app, and make sure both Microphone and Speech Recognition are turned on. Also check that Siri & Dictation is enabled on your device. Without a network connection, recognition works only if your device supports on-device speech recognition.

Is my voice recorded or sent anywhere?

The app does not record or store your voice. Recognition happens on your device when possible; otherwise iOS may send audio to Apple for transcription. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Progress and statistics are saved on your device automatically. Deleting the app removes this data.

Can I restore my stats on a new device?

Data is stored locally and is not synced, so it does not transfer between devices.
